# lomi. Shopify App: Merchant Setup Guide

Welcome to **lomi.**! This guide will help you integrate lomi. payments into your Shopify store, allowing your customers to pay with their preferred local payment methods (Mobile Money, Cards, etc.).

## Prerequisites

- A **Shopify Store** (Basic plan or higher).
- A **lomi.** Merchant Account (verify your business at [dashboard.lomi.africa](https://dashboard.lomi.africa)).

---

## Step 1: Install the App

1.  Navigate to the **Shopify App Store** link provided by lomi. (or your custom install link).
2.  Click **Install**.
3.  You will be redirected to your Shopify Admin. Click **Install app** to grant the necessary permissions.

## Step 2: Configure Your Credentials

To process payments securely, you need to connect your lomi. account.

1.  In your Shopify Admin, go to **Apps** > **lomi.**
2.  You will see the **Settings** page.
3.  **API Key**:
    - Go to your [lomi. Dashboard > Developers](https://dashboard.lomi.africa/developers).
    - Copy your **Public Key** (starts with `pk_live_` or `pk_test_`).
    - Paste it into the **lomi. API Key** field in Shopify.
4.  **Webhook Secret** (Crucial for Order Completion):
    - In your lomi. Dashboard, go to **Webhooks**.
    - Create a new Webhook for the event `PAYMENT_SUCCEEDED`.
    - Set the URL to: `https://<YOUR_SHOPIFY_APP_URL>/api/webhooks/lomi/payment-success` (Contact support if you don't know your App URL).
    - Copy the **Signing Secret** generated by lomi.
    - Paste it into the **lomi. Webhook Secret** field in Shopify.
5.  Click **Save and Validate**.

## Step 3: Enable the Checkout Button

Now you need to place the "Pay with lomi." button on your checkout page.

1.  In Shopify Admin, go to **Settings** (bottom left) > **Checkout**.
2.  Next to **Checkout customization**, click **Customize**.
3.  You are now in the Checkout Editor.
4.  Look for the **App blocks** section (usually on the bottom left sidebar).
5.  Click **Add app block**.
6.  Select **lomi. Checkout**.
7.  The "Pay with lomi." button will appear in the preview. Use the drag handle `⋮⋮` to position it where you want (e.g., above the "Pay now" button or in the Payment section).
8.  Click **Save** in the top right corner.

## Step 4: Go Live!

1.  Make a test purchase on your store.
2.  Click **Pay with lomi.**
3.  Complete the payment.
4.  Verify that:
    - You are redirected to the success page.
    - A new **Order** appears in your Shopify Admin marked as **Paid**.

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Authentication Failed"

- Check that you copied the correct API Key. Ensure there are no extra spaces.
- Ensure you are using the correct mode (Test vs Live).

### Order Not Created in Shopify

- Check your **Webhook Secret**. If this is incorrect, the app cannot verify the payment success message from lomi.
- Ensure your Webhook URL in the lomi. Dashboard is correct.

### Button Not Showing

- Ensure you clicked **Save** in the Checkout Editor.
- Check if your Shopify Plan supports Checkout Extensions (most modern plans do).
